Tamagoyaki (Rolled Omelet) The quintessential bento item! This slightly sweet and savory rolled egg is a favorite for its bright yellow color and fluffy texture. Flavor: Sweet-savory and delicate. Ingredients: 3 eggs, 1 tbsp dashi (or water), 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp soy sauce. Instructions: Whisk ingredients. Pour a thin layer into a hot tamagoyaki pan, roll it up, and repeat until all the egg is used.
